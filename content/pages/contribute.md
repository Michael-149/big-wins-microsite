## What we are looking for

- **Cases we missed.** Social science research that helped change a policy, program, market or practice for large numbers of people, anywhere in the world. We especially want cases from outside the United States, from disciplines other than economics, and cases where the idea began with practitioners or with the people affected.
- **Corrections.** A wrong number, date, name or attribution; a source that does not say what we say it does; a policy whose status has changed; a debate we left out.
- **Near misses and stalled efforts.** Research that looked ready to scale and did not, or programs that worked in trials and faded when they grew. Every case on this site is a success, so these are the stories we most lack.
- **New topic areas.** Fields where a collection like this would be useful, and people who could help build one.

## What to include

A few sentences are enough to start. These details help us follow up:

- **For a case:** what changed, for whom and at roughly what scale; which research played a part and who did it; how the research reached the people who made the change; and links to published sources.
- **For a correction:** the page and passage, what is wrong, what it should say, and a source.
- **For a near miss:** what looked promising, how far it got, what got in the way, and any sources.
- **For a topic area:** the field, why it would be worth covering, a few cases that might belong in it, and people who know it well.

Your name, email and affiliation are optional. If you leave an email, we may write to ask a follow-up question.

## What happens next

We review every submission. Corrections we can confirm go onto the site, with a note on the page for any substantive change.

Suggested cases join our long list of candidates. The strongest may become new case pages, researched and fact-checked to the same standard as the current eight. That takes time, so not every good suggestion will become a page, and we may not reply to each one individually.

We credit contributors who ask to be credited, and no one else. To be credited, tick the box on the form.

## Topics we would like to add

Today's eight cases touch health, housing, education, the environment, household savings and the design of markets, each only thinly. We would like to build fuller collections, field by field, with people who know them well. Some places to start:

- **Public health.** Prevention, insurance design and changes in behavior across whole populations.
- **Public safety.** Policing, courts, violence prevention and reentry after prison.
- **Education.** Early learning, tutoring, school assignment and teacher policy.
- **Global development.** Cash transfers, schooling and health programs in low- and middle-income countries.
- **Public finance.** Taxes, benefits and how governments raise and spend money.
- **Labor and employment.** Job training, job search, wages and how technology changes work.

These are invitations, not plans. If you lead or fund work in one of these fields and would like to help build a collection, choose "Propose a topic area" on the form and tell us.
